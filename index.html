Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA, working with Prof. Aydogan Ozcan on diffractive optics, computational imaging, and deep-learning inverse design.

I develop AI-enabled optical systems that unlock new capabilities in imaging, sensing, and information processing, with applications in optical computing, displays, and digital pathology. Before UCLA I received my B.S. in Optical Sciences from National Central University (2017) and my M.S. in Electro-optical Engineering from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (2019).

My work has appeared in Nature Communications, Light: Science & Applications, Advanced Photonics, and Science Advances. I interned at Meta Reality Labs on deep learning for VR optics, and I am a 2025 Amazon Fellow at the Amazon–UCLA Science Hub.
